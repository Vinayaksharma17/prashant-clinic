Doctor Profile

Meet our consultant surgeon

Dr. Prashant Yadahalli

MBBS, DNB (General Surgery), FMAS, FIAGES
Consultant Laparoscopic, General & Trauma Surgeon

Laparoscopic Surgeries General Surgery Trauma Care
  • MBBS (RGUHS, AMC Vijayapur)
  • DNB General Surgery (St Philomenas Hospital, Bangalore)
  • Former Assistant Professor & Consultant Surgeon (2020–2025) in SDM College Dharwad

Dr. Prashant Yadahalli is a native of Vijayapur. After completing his post-graduation (DNB) from St. Philomena’s Hospital, Bengaluru, he went on to work at SDM Medical College and Hospital for five years. He has extensive experience in general surgery, having performed numerous procedures including inguinal and incisional hernia repairs, surgeries for piles and fistula, complicated laparoscopic appendicectomies, and gallbladder surgeries. In addition, he has successfully completed over 500 endoscopic procedures, including upper GI endoscopy, colonoscopy, and variceal banding. In October 2025, Dr. Yadahalli returned to Vijayapur with the intent of serving the local community. We are eagerly waiting to serve you with dedication, care, and expertise
